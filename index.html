JeongriCut: Photo Cleanup

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

About this policy

JeongriCut: Photo Cleanup is maintained by 0gam-B. This policy covers the Android app, package app.mediacleanup. JeongriCut runs without an account. The ad-free release builds include versions 0.6.5, 0.6.6, and 0.6.7. The advertising section describes builds with AdMob enabled.

Google Play manages app distribution and may process installation and app integrity information under the Google Privacy Policy.

Photos and videos

With your Android permission, the app reads photos and videos, including their file names, dates, sizes, albums, and contents. It uses this information to suggest files to review, display previews, and compare duplicate or similar files. The media and analysis stay on your device.

Use Android Settings to change or revoke media access. On supported Android versions, you can grant access to selected items. Android confirms requests to move items to the trash or delete them. If you choose to open an item in another app, Android grants that app read access to the selected item.

Local storage and reminders

JeongriCut stores review progress, file references, Keep choices, and reminder settings in private app storage. These records help you resume a review and keep the same files from being suggested again. The app excludes its private data from Android backups and device transfers.

If you enable weekly reminders, the app checks your permitted media in the background on your device. The notification can show a count of new suggestions. In versions with a reminder size setting, you can choose the minimum total size of new files that triggers a reminder, and the notification can show that batch’s combined size. The app keeps a local record of suggestions it has already mentioned. Turn reminders off in JeongriCut Settings or manage notifications in Android Settings.

Review state and preferences are kept on the device until they are replaced or you clear app storage or uninstall JeongriCut. To clear them, open Android Settings → Apps → JeongriCut → Storage → Clear storage. Your photo and video files, Android trash, and any backups are managed separately.

Advertising

Ad-enabled builds can show a Google AdMob banner on the cleanup result screen. In those builds, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares your IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and applicable device or account identifiers. Identifiers can include your Android advertising ID and app set ID. Google may use your IP address to estimate your approximate location.

The SDK uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google encrypts SDK data in transit. JeongriCut processes your photos, videos, and review records on the device. Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Google’s User Messaging Platform checks which advertising privacy choices apply when an ad-enabled build opens. It can show a consent message where required. When Privacy choices appears in JeongriCut Settings, you can use it to review or change those choices. Where supported, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings.

Privacy questions and requests
